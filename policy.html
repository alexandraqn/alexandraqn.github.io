Policy & Advisory Experience

Across ten years of international development and policy evaluation work, I have delivered micro-econometric analysis, health financing advisory, and program management for multilateral institutions, government ministries, and international consulting firms. As a Short-Term Consultant for The World Bank Group, I focused on health, poverty, and labor survey microdata harmonization, and served as Resident Advisor for survey design at the Liberian Institute of Statistics and Geo-Information Services (LISGIS). At NIRAS Denmark, I worked primarily as Project Manager and lead tender writer for European Commission framework contracts and public health bids. My advisory track record also includes serving as Senior Associate in Sustainable Health Financing with the Clinton Health Access Initiative (CHAI) in Malawi, alongside econometric consulting roles for the Liberia Revenue Authority and the Ministry of Energy and Mines in Peru.
