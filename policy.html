Policy & Advisory Experience

Across ten years of experience as an economist, I have worked extensively across the international development sector, public sector advisory for national governments in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs), and UK academic research. My work centers on policy advisory, programme evaluation, and leading large-scale microdata projects designed to strengthen evidence-based decision-making. My core technical outputs encompass econometric analysis, official capacity building, health financing planning, and designing administrative data frameworks for macroeconomic and microeconomic policy analysis.

In the private sector, I served as a Programme Manager for European Commission framework projects, leading high-tempo delivery while ensuring long-term skills sustainability within advised governments. I also bring extensive experience writing, bidding for, and winning competitive European Commission tenders. Recently, as a consultant for the World Bank Group, I focused on harmonising national labour force and household survey microdata for the Global Labour Database project. In academic research, I spent two years at the Centre for Health Economics (CHE) at the University of York, applying causal inference and economic evaluation methods to global health projects, building upon earlier experience as a public sector economist in Peru.
