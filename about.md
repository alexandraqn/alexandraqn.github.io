I am a PhD Candidate in Economics (Health Economics Pathway) at the University of York.

My research interests are in health economics and health policy, with a focus on household labour behaviour, and causal inference using applied microeconometric methods.

My work sits at the intersection of policy and quantitative analysis, with the aim of informing evidence-based policy.
