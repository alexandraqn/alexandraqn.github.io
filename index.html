Job Market Paper

JOB MARKET PAPER
The Effect of Public Health Insurance on Labour Supply: Evidence from Indonesia's Askeskin Programme
with Dr. Julia Hatamyar
Abstract: We evaluate the impact of subsidized health insurance expansion on labor supply decisions using national household panel survey microdata from Indonesia (N = 13,000). Applying doubly robust difference-in-differences (DiD) estimation, we find that health insurance coverage mitigates health-related exits from informal sector employment while reducing working hours by 1.3 hours per week among female cohabitants without compromising overall labor force participation.

Working Papers & Research in Progress

  • The Impact of Expanding Child Health Insurance on Parental Labour Supply: Evidence from Mexico's Seguro Popular
    Single-authored Working Paper
    Applies a Regression Discontinuity Design (RDD) at the age eligibility cutoff using 8 consecutive quarterly survey rounds of national Mexican Labour Force Survey (ENOE) microdata. Demonstrates that beneficiary mothers increased labor supply by 2 hours per month while overall employment effects remained neutral.
  • Hospitalisations and Labour Supply: Evidence from Indonesia
    with Dr. Julia Hatamyar and Prof. Luigi Siciliani
    Applies parametric micro-econometric methods and matching techniques to evaluate household hospitalisation shocks, finding a 4-percentage point decrease in female cohabitant employment driven by caregiving burdens.
  • The Relationship Between Insulin Prices and the COMISCA Joint Negotiation Mechanism
    with Prof. R. Moreno-Serra, M. Campos, P. Lagos, and M. García Gonzales
    Deploys an event-study framework on pharmaceutical procurement data across 5 Central American countries.
