About

I am an applied microeconomist completing my PhD in Economics at the University of York (Thesis submitted: Sep 2026; Viva: Dec 7, 2026), supervised by Prof. Luigi Siciliani and Dr. Sumit Mazumdar.

My research primary fields are Applied Micro-econometrics, Health Economics, Labour Economics, and Development Economics, with a focus on quantitative causal inference and program evaluation.

Job Market Paper

Job Market Paper · Single-Authored
The Impact of Expanding Child Health Insurance on Parental Labour Supply: Evidence from Mexico's Seguro Popular
Single-authored Working Paper
  • Identification Strategy: Sharp Regression Discontinuity Design (RDD) executed at the age eligibility cutoff across 8 consecutive quarterly rounds of national Mexican Labour Force Survey microdata (ENOE).
  • Key Empirical Finding: Demonstrates that while overall parental employment effects remain neutral, mothers in beneficiary households increase labor supply by 2 hours per month.
  • Target Journal: World Development
Manuscript, code, and online appendix available via EconJobMarket or upon request.
