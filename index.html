Job Market Paper

JOB MARKET PAPER
The Effect of Public Health Insurance on Labour Supply: Evidence from Indonesia's Askeskin Programme
with Dr. Julia Hatamyar
  • Research Question: Evaluates how subsidized public health insurance expansion influences labor market retention and working hours in developing economy settings.
  • Data & Method: Applied doubly robust difference-in-differences (DiD) estimation on national household survey panel microdata (SUSENAS, N = 13,000).
  • Key Findings: Subsidized coverage mitigates health-related exits from informal sector employment while reducing working hours by 1.3 hours/week among female cohabitants, without distorting overall labor force participation.
  • Target Journal: World Development (Presented at EEA 2026, EuHEA 2026, HESG 2026).
📄 Full manuscript, online appendix, and code available via EconJobMarket or upon email request.

Selected Working Papers & Research

  • The Impact of Expanding Child Health Insurance on Parental Labour Supply: Evidence from Mexico's Seguro Popular
    Single-authored Working Paper
    • Methodology: Sharp Regression Discontinuity Design (RDD) executed at the age eligibility cutoff across 8 consecutive quarterly rounds of Mexican Labour Force Survey (ENOE) microdata.
    • Core Result: Demonstrates that while overall parental employment remains neutral, mothers in beneficiary households increase labor supply by 2 hours per month.
    📄 Draft available upon request.
  • Hospitalisations and Labour Supply: Evidence from Indonesia
    with Dr. Julia Hatamyar and Prof. Luigi Siciliani
    • Methodology: Micro-econometric modeling and matching techniques on panel microdata (SUSENAS).
    • Core Result: Identifies a 4-percentage point decrease in female cohabitant employment driven by household caregiving burdens following health shocks.
